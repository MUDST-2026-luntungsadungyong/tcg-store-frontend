# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Collectors use Mythix to discover, reserve, and manage ownership of TCG cards. Buyers review their account and contact details; sellers use the same area before applying to list cards.

## Product Purpose

Mythix records reservations and card ownership between collectors. It supports the discovery-to-reservation flow while payment, shipping, and contact happen outside the platform.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and collector-focused. The interface should feel deliberate and easy to scan without competing with card content or account tasks.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-styled dashboards, decorative glass surfaces, nested cards, gratuitous icon containers, and low-contrast muted text.

## Design Principles

- Keep account tasks clear, direct, and independently actionable.
- Use familiar product controls and consistent interaction patterns.
- Let information hierarchy, spacing, and contrast create emphasis before ornament.
- Reserve accent colors for meaningful actions and state.
- Preserve a calm dark surface that keeps card and profile content legible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, keep every action keyboard reachable with a visible focus state, and use icons only with text labels or accessible names.
